Basic Font Information

Sainooz EI is a geometric font that is considered as a very Russian font.

Designed by Klasky Wolf Lodge.

Sainooz EI is a Unicode typeface family that supports languages that use
the Latin and Cyrillic script.

More specifically, this release supports the following
Unicode ranges: Latin and Cyrillic
